Belvedir is an observability and auto-improvement platform for LLM agents. Install the SDK, wrap your agent runs in sessions, and Belvedir segments the traces into the tasks your agent performs, gathers similar tasks into task groups, and uses them to route each call to the right model, train smaller models on your own traffic, and propose improvements to your agent's code.

## Set up in this order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get traces flowing">
    The [Quickstart](/quickstart) (Node) or the [Python quickstart](/sdk/python): install, initialize, wrap runs in sessions. Five minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fit it to your stack">
    Guides for [Next.js](/guides/nextjs), [Vercel](/guides/vercel), the [AI SDK](/guides/ai-sdk), [custom agent loops](/guides/custom-agent-loops), [background workers](/guides/background-workers), and [agents without the SDK](/guides/otel-agents). Bring history over with [Importing traces](/importing-traces).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route your calls through Belvedir">
    Two lines change and Belvedir executes your LLM calls, picking the cheapest model that handles each task. Start at [Route your calls](/inference/route-your-calls).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Improve the agent">
    [Optimization loops](/training/optimization-loops) propose changes to your agent's prompts and code. [LoRA finetuning](/training/overview) trains a small model on your successful traffic. [Benchmarks](/benchmarks/overview) measure both.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Trace" href="/sdk/supported-providers">
    Every LLM and tool call from OpenAI, Anthropic, Cohere, Bedrock, Vertex AI, and more. Sessions segment into tasks; similar tasks gather into task groups.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Route" href="/inference/mixture-of-models">
    Send each call to the model that fits it, decided from your own task groups, and let the record of what worked pick the cheapest model that wins.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Optimize" href="/training/optimization-loops">
    Loops that read production traces and open PRs against your repo, plus LoRA finetuning on your successful traffic.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Benchmark" href="/benchmarks/overview">
    Run any agent harness in an isolated sandbox against SWE-bench, τ-bench, GAIA, and more, or against environments generated from your own task groups.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Supported runtimes

The Node and Python SDKs (both named `belvedir`, on npm and PyPI) auto-instrument the major LLM providers. Local models and hosted OpenAI-compatible endpoints work out of the box; see [Local models](/inference/local-models) and [Cloud Inference](/inference/hosted). Coming from Fractal? See [Migrating from Fractal](/migrating-from-fractal).

<Tip>
Working with a coding agent? The dashboard's **Quick Start** page has a **Copy prompt for AI** button. Paste the prompt into your coding agent and it installs the Belvedir skill into your codebase and instruments the app for you. The same guide is at [platform.belvedir.ai/agents.md](https://platform.belvedir.ai/agents.md).
</Tip>
