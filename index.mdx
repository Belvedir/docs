Belvedir traces your LLM agents, segments each run into the tasks the agent performed, and uses those tasks to route calls, fine-tune models, and propose changes to your agent's code. Coming from Fractal? See [Migrating from Fractal](/migrating-from-fractal).

## Set up in this order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get traces flowing">
    The [Quickstart](/quickstart) (Node) or the [Python quickstart](/sdk/python): install, initialize, wrap runs in sessions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fit it to your stack">
    Guides for [Next.js and Vercel](/guides/nextjs), the [AI SDK](/guides/ai-sdk), [custom agent loops](/guides/custom-agent-loops), [background workers](/guides/background-workers), and [agents without the SDK](/guides/otel-agents). Bring history over with [Importing traces](/importing-traces).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route your calls through Belvedir">
    Two lines change and Belvedir executes your LLM calls. Start at [Route your calls](/inference/route-your-calls).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Improve the agent">
    [Optimization loops](/training/optimization-loops) propose changes to your agent's prompts and code. [LoRA finetuning](/training/overview) trains a small model on your successful traffic. [Benchmarks](/benchmarks/overview) measure both.
  </Step>
</Steps>
