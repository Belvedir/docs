Open **Playground** in the sidebar, pick a model, and send a message. Add up to four models and every message goes to all of them at once, so you can compare answers, speed, and cost on the same prompt.

## Comparing models

Each column is one model. Click **Add model** for another column (up to four) and use the picker at the top of a column to change its model at any time. Every column keeps its own conversation: a follow-up replays that model's own earlier answers, and a column added or swapped mid-conversation only sees the turns it answered itself. **Stop** cuts every reply short.

Under each reply, a stats line shows total time and time to first token, input and output tokens, and the cost of that reply as billed to your organization.

## System prompt and parameters

**Parameters** above the columns sets a system message, a temperature (0 to 2), and a maximum reply length for every column. Models that reject sampling controls ignore the temperature; a very low token cap turns a thinking model's reasoning off so the answer fits.

## Which models are listed

The picker lists the hosted models [Belvedir can serve](/inference/models), grouped by provider, plus the endpoints you registered on the **Cloud Inference** page. You can also type any other model identifier the router accepts. With **Chinese models** off under **Project Permissions**, Chinese-lab models are neither listed nor served.

Fine-tuned models run here once you deploy them and register the endpoint on the **Cloud Inference** page; local models never run here.

## Billing

A Playground call is billed to your organization per token at the rates on the Pricing page (**Inference → Pricing**) and appears on the usage page as **Playground**. Calls to your own registered endpoints run on your provider account and cost nothing on Belvedir. The Playground never routes or pins: the model you pick is the model that answers.
