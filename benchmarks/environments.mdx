Environments are distilled from your production traffic — no setup required. Each group of similar tasks, once it has collected 50 or more clean successful examples, doubles as a reproducible setting you can train and evaluate against: the examples define what good output looks like for that kind of work. The Environments page lists which of your groups have crossed the floor and which are still collecting.
